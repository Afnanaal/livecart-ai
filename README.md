# LiveCart AI

## Real-Time Product Intelligence & Q&A

LiveCart AI is a real-time e-commerce intelligence platform that combines
modern data engineering, real-time streaming, Delta Lakehouse architecture,
and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

## Problem

E-commerce product information is distributed across product catalogs,
customer reviews, support content, pricing, and inventory data.

Traditional chatbots may rely on stale or incomplete information, which can
lead to inaccurate answers.

## Solution

LiveCart AI ingests and validates real-time commerce events, processes them
through a Bronze/Silver/Gold Delta Lakehouse, and provides grounded answers
through a RAG pipeline with hybrid search and reranking.
